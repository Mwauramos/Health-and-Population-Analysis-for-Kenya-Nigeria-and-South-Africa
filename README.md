# Health Systems Analysis: Kenya, Nigeria & South Africa

**Author:** Amos Mwaura  
**Analysis Period:** 2010-2024  
**Data Source:** World Bank Health Nutrition and Population Statistics  

## Project Overview
Comprehensive comparative analysis of health systems across three major African economies using 15 years of World Bank data.

## Key Findings
- South Africa leads in per capita health spending
- Nigeria shows concerning underinvestment
- Kenya demonstrates steady improvement
- High out-of-pocket burden across all countries
